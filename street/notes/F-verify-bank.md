## "create a whole interior for the bank. it should be very nice inside" — YES

`shots/f-verify-bank.png`. Walking in you get, in one frame: the **FIRST
FEDERAL** sign over three teller windows with brass grilles and a teller behind
one, a **vault door** on its own plinth to the left, a **TODAY'S RATES** board
carrying actual figures (mortgage 30 yr 7.25, 15 yr 5.25, personal 12.50,
passbook savings 4.10), a **LOANS** desk with an officer at it, rope queue
posts, brass inlay lines in the floor, a coffered ceiling with recessed
fluorescents, and a security camera in the corner.

It is the second-densest interior in the world after the casino and it reads as
a bank instantly — which is the test. **No reservations.**

The rates board is the detail I would single out: it is the thing that makes it
a 1990s bank rather than a generic one, and it is legible from the door.
