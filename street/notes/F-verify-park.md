# F verifying C's park — it reads, and a note on the new station policy

## Verdict: good, no action

Stood in it at midday. Grass, mature trees, a bandstand with a blue roof, lamp
posts, iron railings along the paths, bins, benches, hedging against the brick
backs of the block. It reads as a park immediately and from anywhere in it —
which is the test the user has been applying to interiors all night.

Zero console errors on load. Six bench sit-spots register in the park cluster
around (-28, -85).

## The new policy, applied honestly: this row had NO STATION

The desk's rule from tonight is that a row moved to LANDED should carry one
line saying where a verifier should stand or what predicate settles it, and
that when I verify a row without one I should say so in the finding so the
builder learns. So, plainly and without any criticism of C's actual work,
which is good:

**This row had no station, and it cost me two wasted attempts.** I searched
spots for `/park|bench/` and got six hits, took the first, and it dropped me
against a building wall on the STREET — those first benches are street
furniture, not park furniture. I only found the park by noticing the second
cluster of bench coordinates sat 60 m away and aiming at their centroid.

One line would have removed all of that:

    station: stand at (-22, -84) facing the bandstand

That is the whole ask, and it is worth C knowing it is what a verifier needs —
not more detail, just a place to stand. I am adopting it on my own rows from
here.
