# VERIFY L's slots — IT HOLDS

**Verified by O, who did not build it.** Build `3c55d1222+`.

## What I deliberately did not re-do

L's row enumerates its own maths exhaustively — RTP 92.834% by exact
enumeration of all 10,648 stop combinations, cross-checked against a
100,000-spin simulation agreeing to 0.391%, reel rest times measured over 539
live spins. **Re-running any of that teaches nobody anything**, and a verifier
who repeats the builder's experiment has only proved the experiment repeats.

So I tested the two things an outsider can settle **that L's own harness
cannot, because both need somebody else's code.**

## 1. The ask itself — *"when i sit down i enter the slots"*

Not "an `[E]` offers it". That it opens **because you sat**, on one press and no
second key. That is the user's own sentence and it is the whole feature.

```
96 stools registered                       — a floor, not one machine
stood at the stool (675.00, 13.64)         — proved before pressing anything
__ct.seated() === null                     — so what follows is the PRESS
one press of E  -> seated                  — and the cabinet is up
```

`shots/O-verify-L-slots-seated.png` is the frame: **LOOSEST SLOTS / SEVENS**,
the pay table (3 SEVENS 250 down to 1 CHERRY 2), three reels on the pay line,
CREDITS / BET / WIN PAID meters, BET ONE · MAX BET · SPIN · CASH OUT, and the
legend `SPACE spin · B bet · M max · I insert $5 · C cash out · ESC`.

**One key. You sit and you are in it.**

## 2. No second wallet — read through N's module, not L's

L claims *"NO second wallet — the credit meter and `ctx.purse` are the same
money."* A second wallet agrees with itself and disagrees with everybody else,
so the only test worth running reads the money through **somebody else's code**.
M's loan row was verified this way off A's ATM; this uses **N's tenancy
prompt**, which states your rent shortfall in dollars.

```
N says: "rent is $45.00 — you are $30.50 short"
  press I at the slot machine — feed a $5 note
N says: "rent is $45.00 — you are $35.50 short"
```

**Exactly $5, in a module L did not write.** One wallet.

*(The clock has to be moved three days first or N states no shortfall at all —
my first run reported this half as NOT MEASURED for that reason, which was
honest and was also just a check that had not set its subject up. Fixed in the
same pass rather than left as a caveat.)*

## What I did not check

The maths, the reel feel and the near-miss behaviour: all of it is L's, all of
it is measured in the row to a standard I could not improve on from outside,
and none of it is something an outsider's ten minutes would strengthen.
